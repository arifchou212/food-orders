1. About this Privacy Notice

This Privacy Notice explains how Euro Asia Homemade Foods collects, uses and protects personal information when you use our website or place an order with us.

2. Information we collect

When you place an order, we may collect the following information:

  • Your name
  • Your telephone number
  • The food items you order
  • The quantity of each item ordered
  • The price of the items at the time of ordering
  • Your total order amount
  • Your selected collection date and time slot
  • Your order status
  • The date and time your order is created, where recorded by the application

3. Why we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Process and manage your order
  • Prepare the food you have ordered
  • Organise your collection time
  • Contact you about your order when necessary
  • Answer questions or resolve problems relating to an order
  • Maintain records of orders and business transactions

4. How we use your telephone number

Your telephone number may be used to contact you about your order, including where clarification or an issue relating to collection is required.

We do not use your telephone number for unrelated marketing unless we have an appropriate lawful basis and, where required, your consent.

5. Legal basis for processing

We process information where it is necessary to take steps at your request before an order is completed and to fulfil the order you have placed.

We may also process information where necessary to comply with legal obligations or for legitimate business purposes, where applicable.

6. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as it is reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including order management, record keeping and any applicable legal or accounting requirements.

We will review the information we hold and remove or anonymise information when it is no longer required, where appropriate.

7. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be accessed by people involved in preparing and managing your order where this is necessary to fulfil the order.

We may also use service providers that are necessary to operate the website or provide related services. Where this happens, we expect appropriate safeguards to be used to protect personal information.

8. Website security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or loss.

However, no website or method of transmitting information over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Cookies and sessions

The website may use essential cookies or session information to provide functionality such as maintaining your basket while you use the website.

We do not intend to use non-essential tracking or advertising cookies unless the website is changed to use them in the future.

10. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights under UK data protection law, including rights to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of information where applicable
  • Request restriction of processing in certain circumstances
  • Object to certain processing
  • Request data portability where applicable

These rights are subject to certain legal conditions and exemptions.

11. Contact us about privacy

If you have a question about how we use your personal information or want to exercise a data protection right, please contact us using the details on our Contact page.

12. Changes to this notice

We may update this Privacy Notice when our website, services or data-processing practices change.

The latest version will be made available on this page.
