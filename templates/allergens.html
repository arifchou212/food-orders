Important information

If you have a food allergy or intolerance, please contact us before placing an order.

We will provide information about the ingredients and allergens present in the food we prepare.

Please do not place an order if you are unsure whether a food is suitable for you. If you have a severe allergy, discuss your requirements with us before ordering.

Allergens

The following are the 14 allergens that must be declared when they are used as an ingredient in food:

  • Celery
  • Cereals containing gluten
  • Crustaceans
  • Eggs
  • Fish
  • Lupin
  • Milk
  • Molluscs
  • Mustard
  • Nuts
  • Peanuts
  • Sesame
  • Soya
  • Sulphur dioxide and sulphites

Our food

Allergen information for individual dishes should be checked before ordering.

Cross-contact

Although we take care when preparing food, ingredients containing allergens may be handled in the same preparation environment.

Customers with allergies should contact us before ordering so that we can discuss their requirements.
